# Privacy Policy — ID Tjek

_Last updated: 4 June 2026_

ID Tjek scans an AltID proof-of-age QR code and tells the operator whether the
holder is 18 (or 21) or older. It is built to collect as little as possible.

## What we collect

**Nothing leaves your device.** ID Tjek has no servers, no accounts, and no
analytics or tracking.

- **Camera** — used only to read the QR code in front of it. Frames are
  processed live to verify the code and are never recorded, stored, or sent
  anywhere.
- **Scan results** — the recent-scans list (verdict + timestamp) is kept in
  memory only. It is cleared when you close the app and is never written to
  disk or transmitted.

We never see, store, or transmit the holder's name, birth date, document, or
any personal data. The QR only proves an age threshold (e.g. "over 18"); the
underlying identity is never disclosed to the app.

## Permissions

- **Camera** — required to scan the QR code. The app requests no other
  permissions.

## Third parties

None. ID Tjek does not use third-party SDKs that collect data, and shares data
with no one.
